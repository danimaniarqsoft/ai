Is a grammatical term that describes the role a word plays in a sentence. It's also known as word class.

The eight parts of speech in English are:

- **Noun**: A name of a person, place, organization, thing, or idea 
- **Pronoun**: Takes the place of a noun 
- **Verb**: An action word 
- **Adjective**: Modifies nouns and pronouns 
- **Adverb**: Modifies verbs, adjectives, and adverbs 
- **Preposition**: Describes relationships between nouns and pronouns 
- **Conjunction**: Connects different words and phrases 
- **Interjection**: Breaks the flow of a sentence to demonstrate emotion
